A fresh approach to security in Second Life. Security Panels by Wild Designs.

[WD] Secure watches your land, warns first, then removes only the people who do not belong. Neighbors and flyers keep the airspace.

It ships as pre-assembled Panels. Panels are made to look like decor, so they can sit in a home or yard and still watch the land. Same engine.

## Who it is for

- Mainland and private estate owners who are tired of ban lines
- Linden Home owners who still want strangers off the parcel
- Anyone who wants staff lists, zones, and a clean update path

## What it does

- Works on mainland, private estate, and Linden Home
- Detects the parcel type and locks features that would break that land
- Scan never leaves your parcel
- Watch is zones only. Nothing is scanned until you add a zone
- Range sphere or Custom Zone (two corner markers)
- Warn, then eject (or teleport home on land that allows it)
- Access lists: Owner, Leader, Director, Visitor, Temp Visitor, Restricted
- Arrival box when someone new walks in
- Owner Menu HUD
- Transfer copies lists and zones to another [WD] Secure Panel in the same region
- In-world Updater for script updates. Lists and zones stay

## Linden Home

The Panel stays compliant on Linden Homes.

- Eject only. No Send Home
- No parcel ban list
- No Lockdown
- No Full Parcel zone
- Range cap 200 m
- No new zones between 400 m and 2000 m
- Watch ignores that airspace

## First few minutes

1. Rez the Panel on land you own or can eject from
2. Touch it
3. Zones > Add. Make one Range or Custom Zone
4. Arm

Red = Disarmed. Green = Armed. Orange = no eject rights. Yellow = Lockdown (not on Linden Homes).

## Lists in short

- Owner: full control
- Leader: full menus, zones, Update, Transfer
- Director: Arm, Access, Lockdown where allowed
- Visitor: may stay
- Temp Visitor: stays until midnight SLT
- Restricted: removed and kept off the land
